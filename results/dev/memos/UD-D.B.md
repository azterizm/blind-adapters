# UD-D, arm B: uk_unfair_dismissal
Status: ANSWERED WITH FLAGS

## dismissed: YES [grounded]
Under section 95(1)(a), an employee is dismissed if the contract is terminated by the employer. The employee was dismissed with immediate effect, indicating the contract was terminated by the employer. Section 95(1)(c) also applies if the employee terminates the contract due to the employer's conduct, but in this case, the employer terminated the contract. The effective date of termination under section 97(1)(a) is the date the notice expires, but since no notice was given, the effective date is the date of termination, which is 2 February 2026. Therefore, the employee was dismissed by the employer within the statutory meaning.
  > "She was dismissed with immediate effect on 2 February 2026 for alleged gross misconduct."
  authority: uk/ukpga/1996/18/s95, uk/ukpga/1996/18/s97

## automatic_unfair_reason: UNCLEAR [ungrounded]
Section 108(3) of the Employment Rights Act 1996 disapplies the two-year qualifying period if the dismissal is due to a reason specified in sections 99, 100, or 103A. However, the client's dismissal was for alleged gross misconduct, which does not fall under any of these sections. The facts do not indicate that the dismissal was for pregnancy, health and safety, or a protected disclosure.
