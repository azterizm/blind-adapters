# dismissal, arm T: uk_unfair_dismissal
Status: ANSWERED (every step computed or grounded)

## dismissed: YES [grounded]
Under section 95(1)(a), the employee is dismissed by the employer if the contract is terminated by the employer. The employer terminated the contract on 13 March 2026, and the employee was informed of the dismissal with immediate effect. The effective date of termination is the date the notice expires, which in this case is the date of termination itself as no notice was given. Therefore, the employee was dismissed within the statutory meaning of section 95.
  > "On 12 March 2026 her manager, Dave Pritchard, telephoned her and told her she was dismissed with immediate effect for gross misconduct"
  > "She received a short dismissal letter dated 13 March 2026"
  authority: uk/ukpga/1996/18/s95

## automatic_unfair_reason: NO [grounded]
The dismissal was for gross misconduct due to unauthorised absence, which does not fall under the automatically unfair reasons in sections 99, 100, or 103A. Therefore, section 108(3) does not disapply the two-year qualifying period. The dismissal was not for pregnancy, health and safety, or protected disclosure.

## fairness: YES [grounded]
Under section 98(1), the employer must establish the reason for the dismissal and demonstrate that it is a statutory reason or a substantial reason. The employer dismissed Adaeze Okafor for gross misconduct (three days' unauthorised absence) without an investigation or disciplinary process, which may not meet the criteria for a fair reason. Additionally, section 98(4) requires the tribunal to assess whether the employer acted reasonably in treating the reason as sufficient for dismissal, which is not clearly established in this case.
