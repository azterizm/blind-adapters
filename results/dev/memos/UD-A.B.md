## dismissed: YES [grounded]
Under section 95(1)(a), the employee is dismissed if the contract is terminated by the employer. The employer gave notice of termination, and the contract ended when the notice expired. Section 95(2) also applies as the employer gave notice to terminate, and the employee gave notice to terminate on a date earlier than the employer’s notice. The effective date of termination is the date the notice expired, as per section 97(1)(a).

## automatic_unfair_reason: NO [grounded]

The facts do not indicate that the dismissal was for pregnancy, maternity, health and safety, or protected disclosure reasons. The dismissal was for alleged poor performance, which does not fall under the automatically unfair categories.

## fairness: YES [grounded]
Under section 98(1) of the Employment Rights Act 1996, the employer must show a fair reason for dismissal. The employer's reason (poor performance) must be a 'substantial reason' justifying the dismissal. However, the employer did not provide any evidence of poor performance, warnings, or performance reviews, which are necessary to establish a fair reason. Section 98(4) further requires the employer to act reasonably in treating the reason as sufficient. Since the employer failed to demonstrate a fair reason or show that the reason was sufficient, the dismissal may be unfair.
