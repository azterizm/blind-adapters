## fairness: YES [grounded]
Under section 98(1), the employer must show a potentially fair reason for dismissal falling within section 98(2) or some other substantial reason. Corvid Software Ltd dismissed Mr Bell for alleged poor performance without any warnings or performance reviews, and cannot establish a fair reason under section 98(2)(a) or demonstrate that it acted reasonably under section 98(4). Consequently, the dismissal is unfair.
