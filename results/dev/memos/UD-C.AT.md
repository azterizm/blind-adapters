## dismissed: YES [grounded]
Under section 95(1)(a), an employee is dismissed where the contract under which he is employed is terminated by the employer, whether with or without notice. Mr Hartley was dismissed with immediate effect on 31 March 2026, which constitutes a summary termination without notice. Under section 97(1)(b), the effective date of termination where a contract is terminated without notice is the date on which the termination takes effect.
  > "He was dismissed with immediate effect on 31 March 2026 after a dispute with a customer."

## fairness: UNCLEAR [unclear]
Under section 98(1), the employer must establish the reason for the dismissal and show that it falls within a statutory category or is some other substantial reason. Under section 98(4), the tribunal must determine whether the employer acted reasonably or unreasonably in treating that reason as sufficient to dismiss. The account only states that the dismissal followed a dispute with a customer without providing the employer's evidence or the tribunal's evaluation of reasonableness.
  > "He was dismissed with immediate effect on 31 March 2026 after a dispute with a customer."
