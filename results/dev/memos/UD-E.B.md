## automatic_unfair_reason: UNCLEAR [ungrounded]
Section 108(3) of the Employment Rights Act 1996 disapplies the two-year qualifying period if the dismissal is due to a reason specified in sections 99, 100, or 103A. However, the account does not provide evidence that the dismissal was for a reason covered by these sections. The dismissal was described as being due to a role restructuring, which is not listed as an automatically unfair reason under the specified sections. Therefore, the two-year qualifying period would apply.
