## dismissed: YES [grounded]
Under section 95(1)(a), an employee is dismissed where the contract under which they are employed is terminated by the employer, whether with or without notice. Brightwater Hotels Ltd terminated Ms Fontaine's employment with immediate effect on 28 May 2026 without notice, satisfying the statutory definition of dismissal.

## fairness: UNCLEAR [unclear]
Under section 98(1), the employer must establish the reason for the dismissal and show that it falls within a statutory category or is some other substantial reason. Furthermore, under section 98(4), fairness depends on whether the employer acted reasonably or unreasonably in treating that reason as sufficient to dismiss. The account only states that the dismissal followed a guest complaint without providing the employer's evidence or the tribunal's determination of reasonableness.
