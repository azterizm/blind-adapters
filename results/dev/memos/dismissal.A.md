# dismissal, arm A: uk_unfair_dismissal
Status: ANSWERED (every step computed or grounded)

## dismissed: YES [grounded]
Under section 95(1)(a), an employee is dismissed if the contract under which she is employed is terminated by the employer, whether with or without notice. Ms Okafor was dismissed with immediate effect by her employer on 13 March 2026, which constitutes a summary dismissal without notice taking effect on that date pursuant to section 97(1)(b).
  > "On 12 March 2026 her manager, Dave Pritchard, telephoned her and told her she was dismissed with immediate effect for gross misconduct, namely three days' unauthorised absence from 16 to 18 February 2026."
  > "She received a short dismissal letter dated 13 March 2026."
  authority: uk/ukpga/1996/18/s95, uk/ukpga/1996/18/s97

## fairness: YES [grounded]
Under section 98(1), the employer must show a potentially fair reason for dismissal falling within section 98(2) or some other substantial reason. Gross misconduct falls within section 98(2)(b), but the employer acted unreasonably under section 98(4) by dismissing Ms Okafor summarily without any investigation, disciplinary hearing, or right of appeal.
  > "No investigation meeting or disciplinary hearing took place and she was not offered an appeal."
  authority: uk/ukpga/1996/18/s98
