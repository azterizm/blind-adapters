## by_reason_of_redundancy: YES [grounded]
Under section 139(1)(a)(ii), a dismissal is by reason of redundancy if it is wholly or mainly attributable to the employer ceasing or intending to cease carrying on the business in the place where the employee was employed. Halcyon Print Ltd closed the Leicester site where Tomasz Wisniewski worked because the company was moving all printing to its Glasgow site. His dismissal was therefore wholly or mainly attributable to the closure of the Leicester site.
  > "On 30 April 2026 he received written notice that the Leicester site would close because the company is moving all printing to its Glasgow site, and that his employment would end on 29 May 2026."
  authority: uk/ukpga/1996/18/s139

## suitable_alternative: YES [grounded]
Under section 141(2), an employee loses entitlement to a redundancy payment only if he unreasonably refuses an offer of renewal or re-engagement. In this case, the employer did not offer Tomasz Wisniewski any alternative role in Glasgow or elsewhere. Because no offer was made, he did not unreasonably refuse an offer and retains his right to a redundancy payment.
  > "He was not offered a role in Glasgow or anywhere else."
  authority: uk/ukpga/1996/18/s141
