# redundancy, arm B: uk_redundancy_payment

## dismissed: YES [grounded]
Under section 136(1)(a), an employee is dismissed by the employer if the contract is terminated by the employer. The employee was informed that the Leicester site would close, and his employment would end on 29 May 2026, which constitutes termination of the contract by the employer due to redundancy. The notice given by the employer to terminate the contract meets the statutory definition of dismissal under section 136.

## suitable_alternative: YES [grounded]
Under section 141, the employee is not entitled to a redundancy payment if he unreasonably refuses an offer of suitable employment. In this case, the employee was not offered a role in Glasgow or anywhere else, so there was no suitable offer. Therefore, the employee is entitled to a redundancy payment.
  > "He was not offered a role in Glasgow or anywhere else."
  authority: uk/ukpga/1996/18/s141
