# UD-C, arm B: uk_unfair_dismissal
Status: ANSWERED (every step computed or grounded)

## dismissed: YES [grounded]
Under section 95(1)(a), an employee is dismissed if the contract is terminated by the employer. The employee was dismissed with immediate effect, indicating the contract was terminated by the employer. Section 95(1)(c) also applies if the employee terminates the contract due to the employer's conduct, but in this case, the employer terminated the contract. The effective date of termination under section 97(1)(a) is the date the notice expires, but since no notice was given, the effective date is the date of termination, which is March 31, 2026. Therefore, the dismissal falls under section 95(1)(a).
  > "He was dismissed with immediate effect on 31 March 2026 after a dispute with a customer."
  authority: uk/ukpga/1996/18/s95, uk/ukpga/1996/18/s97

## fairness: NO [grounded]
Under section 98(1) of the Employment Rights Act 1996, the employer must show a reason for the dismissal and that it is a fair reason. The employer's reason for dismissing Owen Hartley was a dispute with a customer, which does not fall under the listed fair reasons in section 98(2). Therefore, the employer has not demonstrated a fair reason for the dismissal.
