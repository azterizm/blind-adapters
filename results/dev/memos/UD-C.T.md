## dismissed: YES [grounded]
Under section 95(1)(a), an employee is dismissed if the contract is terminated by the employer. The employee was dismissed with immediate effect on 31 March 2026, which constitutes termination by the employer. Section 95(2) also covers scenarios where the employer gives notice and the employee serves counter-notice, but in this case, the dismissal was immediate, not during a notice period. Therefore, the employee was dismissed within the statutory meaning.
