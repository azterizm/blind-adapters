# NV-CD2, arm AT: uk_unfair_dismissal
Status: ANSWERED (every step computed or grounded)

## dismissed: YES [grounded]
Under section 95(1)(c), an employee is dismissed if they terminate the contract under which they are employed (with or without notice) in circumstances where they are entitled to terminate it without notice by reason of the employer's conduct. The employer unilaterally imposed a new shift pattern without notice, and Mr Kowalczyk resigned in response to this conduct. Under section 95(2), where the employer gives notice to terminate the contract and the employee serves counter-notice to terminate it on an earlier date, the employee is treated as dismissed.

## fairness: NO [grounded]
Under section 98(1), the employer must establish the principal reason for dismissal and show that it falls within a statutory category or is some other substantial reason. Here, the employer sought to change Mr Kowalczyk's shift pattern, which does not constitute a statutory reason for dismissal. Furthermore, under section 98(4), the dismissal cannot be fair where the employer acted reasonably in treating the reason as sufficient to dismiss, and the employer cannot establish a fair reason for the dismissal.
