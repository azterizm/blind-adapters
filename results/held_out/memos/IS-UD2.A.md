# IS-UD2, arm A: uk_unfair_dismissal
Status: ANSWERED (every step computed or grounded)

## dismissed: YES [grounded]
Under section 95(1)(a), an employee is dismissed if the contract under which he is employed is terminated by the employer, whether with or without notice. Mr Reid was dismissed with immediate effect on 19 June 2026 without notice, which constitutes a summary dismissal taking effect on that date under section 97(1)(b).

## fairness: YES [grounded]
Under section 98(1), the employer must show a potentially fair reason for dismissal falling within section 98(2) or some other substantial reason. A hygiene inspection failure does not constitute a potentially fair statutory reason under section 98(2). Furthermore, under section 98(4), the employer acted unreasonably in treating a hygiene inspection failure as a sufficient reason to dismiss an employee summarily without notice.
  > "He was dismissed with immediate effect on 19 June 2026 after his manager said the kitchen had failed a hygiene inspection."
