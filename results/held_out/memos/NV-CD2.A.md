# NV-CD2, arm A: uk_unfair_dismissal
Status: ANSWERED (every step computed or grounded)

## dismissed: YES [grounded]
Under section 95(1)(c), an employee is dismissed if they terminate the contract of employment in circumstances where they are entitled to terminate it without notice by reason of the employer's conduct. The employer unilaterally imposed a significant change to Mr Kowalczyk's shift pattern without agreement, prompting him to resign. Under section 97(1)(a), the effective date of termination is the date on which his notice expired, namely 21 August 2026.
  > "On 3 August 2026 the employer gave him four weeks' written notice that he would move from day shifts to early-morning shifts, with no change in pay."
  > "He did not like the new hours and resigned on 14 August 2026, giving one week's notice; his last day was 21 August 2026."

## fairness: NO [grounded]
Under section 98(1), the employer must show a potentially fair reason for dismissal falling within section 98(2) or some other substantial reason. Resigning in protest over a shift pattern change does not constitute a potentially fair reason for dismissal. Furthermore, under section 98(4), the employer cannot act reasonably in treating a resignation as a sufficient reason to dismiss.
