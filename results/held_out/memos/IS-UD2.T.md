## dismissed: YES [grounded]

Callum Reid resigned on 19 June 2026 after his manager cited a hygiene inspection failure, which constitutes the employer's conduct. Therefore, he is treated as dismissed by the employer.

## fairness: UNCLEAR [unclear]
Section 98(1) requires the employer to establish the reason for dismissal and demonstrate it is a statutory reason or a substantial reason. The employer must also show they acted reasonably in treating that reason as sufficient. The facts indicate the dismissal was due to a hygiene inspection, but it is unclear if this constitutes a statutory reason (such as conduct, capability, redundancy, or statutory restriction) or a substantial reason. Additionally, the employer's reasonableness in treating the hygiene inspection as sufficient for dismissal is not established in the account.
