## dismissed: YES [grounded]
Under section 95(1)(c), an employee is dismissed if they terminate the contract under which they are employed (with or without notice) in circumstances where they are entitled to terminate it without notice by reason of the employer's conduct. Ms Dunmore resigned without notice on 4 September 2026 in direct response to the employer's demotion, accusation of dishonesty, and failure to reply to her written complaint. Under section 95(2), where the employer gives notice to terminate the contract and the employee serves counter-notice to terminate it on an earlier date, the employee is treated as dismissed.

## fairness: YES [grounded]
Under section 98(1), the employer must establish the principal reason for the dismissal and show that it falls within a statutory category or is some other substantial reason. Ashgrove Estate Agents Ltd dismissed Ms Dunmore without any legitimate reason, and the employer acted unreasonably under section 98(4) by demoting her, accusing her of dishonesty, and failing to respond to her written complaint. Consequently, the dismissal is unfair.
