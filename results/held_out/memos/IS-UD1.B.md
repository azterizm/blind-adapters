## fairness: YES [grounded]
Under section 98(1) of the Employment Rights Act 1996, the employer must show a reason for the dismissal and that it is a fair reason. The employer failed to conduct an investigation or disciplinary hearing, which suggests they may not have a valid reason for the dismissal. Section 98(4) further requires the employer to act reasonably in treating the reason as sufficient, which was not demonstrated in this case.
