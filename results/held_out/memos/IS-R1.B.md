# IS-R1, arm B: uk_redundancy_payment
Status: ANSWERED (every step computed or grounded)

## dismissed: YES [grounded]
Under section 136(1)(a), an employee is dismissed by the employer if the contract is terminated by the employer. In this case, the employer gave written notice that the company was closing its Coventry factory and that Ms Okoye's employment would end on 30 September 2026. This constitutes the employer terminating the contract, which falls within the statutory definition of dismissal.
  > "On 1 September 2026 she was given written notice that the company was closing its Coventry factory and that her employment would end on 30 September 2026; it ended on that date."
  authority: uk/ukpga/1996/18/s136

## suitable_alternative: YES [grounded]
Under section 141, the employee is not entitled to a redundancy payment if she unreasonably refuses an offer of suitable employment. In this case, the employee was not offered any other job, so there was no offer to renew the contract or re-engage her. Therefore, she is entitled to a redundancy payment.
  > "She was not offered any other job."
  > "Her employment would end on 30 September 2026."
  authority: uk/ukpga/1996/18/s141
