Under section 136(1)(a), an employee is dismissed by his employer if the contract under which he is employed is terminated by the employer, whether with or without notice. ## by_reason_of_redundancy: YES [grounded]
Under section 139(1)(b) of the Employment Rights Act 1996, an employee is dismissed by reason of redundancy if the dismissal is wholly or mainly attributable to the fact that the requirements of the business for employees to carry out work of a particular kind have ceased or diminished. Tidewell Logistics Ltd informed Mr Achterberg that it had lost its main contract and needed fewer warehouse staff, and dismissed him for redundancy. Consequently, the dismissal was wholly or mainly attributable to the diminished requirements for employees to carry out that work.
  > "On 11 September 2026 the company told him it had lost its main contract and needed fewer warehouse staff, and dismissed him for redundancy with immediate effect, without notice."
  authority: uk/ukpga/1996/18/s139
