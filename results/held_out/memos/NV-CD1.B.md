# NV-CD1, arm B: uk_unfair_dismissal
Status: REFUSE-AND-FLAG: precondition 'dismissed' failed

## dismissed: NO [grounded]
Under section 95(1)(c), an employee terminates the contract without notice due to the employer's conduct. However, the employee must be entitled to terminate without notice by reason of the employer's conduct. In this case, the employee resigned due to the demotion and accusation of dishonesty, which may constitute the employer's conduct. However, the question is whether the employee was dismissed by the employer, which requires the employer to have terminated the contract. Since the employee terminated the contract, it does not meet the statutory definition of dismissal by the employer under section 95(1)(a) or (b).
  > "On 1 July 2026 the managing director moved her to a junior negotiator role, took her team away from her and, at a staff meeting, accused her of dishonesty without giving any reason."
  > "Her contract does not allow the employer to change her role and she did not agree to the move."
  > "She complained in writing and received no reply."
  > "She resigned without notice on 4 September 2026 because of the demotion and the accusation."
