## dismissed: YES [grounded]
Under section 95(1)(a), an employee is dismissed by her employer if the contract under which she is employed is terminated by the employer, whether with or without notice. Brightline Dental Ltd terminated Ms Marchetti's employment with immediate effect on 24 August 2026. Pursuant to section 97(1)(b), the effective date of termination is the date on which that termination took effect, meaning she has been dismissed within the statutory definition.

## automatic_unfair_reason: YES [grounded]
Under section 99(1) and (3) of the Employment Rights Act 1996, a dismissal is unfair if the reason or principal reason relates to pregnancy, childbirth, or maternity leave. Consequently, section 108(3)(b) disapplies the two-year qualifying period set out in section 108(1).

## fairness: YES [grounded]
Under section 98(1), the employer bears the burden of establishing that the dismissal was for a potentially fair reason falling within section 98(2) or some other substantial reason justifying dismissal. The stated reason—that the employer could not cover the employee's maternity leave—does not relate to capability, qualifications, conduct, redundancy, or statutory contravention under section 98(2), nor is it a substantial reason justifying dismissal under section 98(1)(b). Furthermore, dismissing an employee because they require maternity leave is unreasonable under section 98(4), rendering the dismissal unfair.
