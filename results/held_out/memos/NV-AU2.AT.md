## dismissed: YES [grounded]
Under section 95(1)(a), an employee is dismissed if the contract under which he is employed is terminated by the employer, whether with or without notice. The area manager dismissed Mr Okonkwo with immediate effect on 9 September 2026, which constitutes a summary dismissal without notice. Under section 97(1)(b), the effective date of termination for a dismissal without notice is the date on which the termination takes effect.

## automatic_unfair_reason: YES [grounded]

Under section 103A, a dismissal is automatically unfair where the reason is that the employee made a protected disclosure. Consequently, section 108(3)(ff) disapplies the two-year continuous employment requirement under section 108(1).

## fairness: YES [grounded]
Under section 98(1), the employer must establish the reason for the dismissal and show that it falls within a statutory category or is some other substantial reason. The employer gave no reason for the dismissal other than a personal rebuke, failing to establish any potentially fair reason. Furthermore, under section 98(4), the employer acted unreasonably in treating that reason as sufficient to dismiss.
