## by_reason_of_redundancy: YES [grounded]
Under section 139(1)(a)(ii) of the Employment Rights Act 1996, an employee is dismissed by reason of redundancy if the dismissal is wholly or mainly attributable to the employer ceasing or intending to cease to carry on the business in the place where the employee was employed. Consequently, the dismissal is directly attributable to the employer ceasing business in the place where she worked.
