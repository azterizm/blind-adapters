# NV-AU2, arm A: uk_unfair_dismissal
Status: ANSWERED WITH FLAGS

## dismissed: YES [grounded]
Under section 95(1)(a), an employee is dismissed if the contract under which he is employed is terminated by the employer, whether with or without notice. The area manager dismissed Mr Okonkwo with immediate effect on 9 September 2026, which constitutes a summary dismissal without notice. Under section 97(1)(b), the effective date of termination for a contract terminated without notice is the date on which the termination takes effect.
  > "On 9 September 2026 the area manager dismissed him with immediate effect and told him that people who go over her head do not last here."

## automatic_unfair_reason: YES [grounded]
Mr Okonkwo was dismissed for reporting to his superior that out-of-date controlled drugs were being dispensed to patients, which he believed was unlawful and put patients at risk. Under section 103A, a dismissal is automatically unfair where the reason or principal reason is that the employee made a protected disclosure. Under section 108(3)(ff), the two-year continuous employment qualifying period does not apply where section 103A applies.
  > "On 2 September 2026 he emailed the superintendent pharmacist to report that out-of-date controlled drugs were being dispensed to patients at his branch, which he believed was unlawful and put patients at risk."
  > "On 9 September 2026 the area manager dismissed him with immediate effect and told him that people who go over her head do not last here. No other reason was given."

## fairness: YES [grounded]
Under section 98(1), the employer must show a potentially fair reason for dismissal falling within section 98(2) or some other substantial reason. The employer gave no legitimate reason for the dismissal other than a personal rebuke, failing to establish a fair reason. Furthermore, under section 98(4), the employer acted unreasonably in treating that rebuke as a sufficient reason to dismiss the employee.
  > "On 9 September 2026 the area manager dismissed him with immediate effect and told him that people who go over her head do not last here. No other reason was given."
