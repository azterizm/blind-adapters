## dismissed: YES [grounded]
Under section 95(1)(a), an employee is dismissed by their employer if the contract under which they are employed is terminated by the employer, whether with or without notice. The client's account confirms that the employer's area manager dismissed him with immediate effect on 9 September 2026. ## fairness: YES [grounded]
Under section 98(1), the burden is on the employer to show a potentially fair statutory reason falling within subsection (2) or some other substantial reason justifying dismissal. Riverside Pharmacy Group Ltd dismissed Mr Okonkwo solely because 'people who go over her head do not last here', which does not establish a valid capability, conduct, redundancy, or statutory restriction reason under section 98(2). Even if categorized as conduct or some other substantial reason, dismissing him summarily without procedure for raising safety concerns constitutes acting unreasonably in treating it as a sufficient reason for dismissal under section 98(4).
  > "On 9 September 2026 the area manager dismissed him with immediate effect and told him that people who go over her head do not last here."
  > "No other reason was given."
  authority: uk/ukpga/1996/18/s98
