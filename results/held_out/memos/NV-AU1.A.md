# NV-AU1, arm A: uk_unfair_dismissal
Status: ANSWERED WITH FLAGS

## dismissed: YES [grounded]
Under section 95(1)(a), an employee is dismissed if the contract under which she is employed is terminated by the employer, whether with or without notice. Brightline Dental Ltd dismissed Ms Marchetti with immediate effect on 24 August 2026, which constitutes a summary dismissal without notice. Under section 97(1)(b), the effective date of termination for a contract terminated without notice is the date on which the termination takes effect.
  > "On 24 August 2026 the practice manager dismissed her with immediate effect; the dismissal letter says that the practice cannot cover her maternity leave next year."

## automatic_unfair_reason: YES [grounded]
Under section 99(1), a dismissal is automatically unfair if the reason or principal reason relates to pregnancy, childbirth, or maternity. Brightline Dental Ltd dismissed Ms Marchetti immediately after she disclosed her pregnancy, citing an inability to cover her maternity leave. Under section 108(3)(b), the two-year continuous employment requirement in section 108(1) does not apply where section 99(1) applies.
  > "On 3 August 2026 she told the practice manager that she was pregnant."

## fairness: YES [grounded]
Under section 98(1), the employer must show a potentially fair reason for dismissal falling within section 98(2) or some other substantial reason. Dismissing an employee because she cannot cover maternity leave is not a potentially fair reason under section 98(2), and the employer acted unreasonably under section 98(4) in treating it as a sufficient reason to dismiss.
  > "On 24 August 2026 the practice manager dismissed her with immediate effect; the dismissal letter says that the practice cannot cover her maternity leave next year."
  authority: uk/ukpga/1996/18/s98
