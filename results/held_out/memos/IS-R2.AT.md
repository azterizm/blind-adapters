## dismissed: YES [grounded]
Under section 136(1)(a), an employee is dismissed if the contract under which he is employed is terminated by the employer, whether with or without notice. ## by_reason_of_redundancy: YES [grounded]
Under section 139(1), a dismissal is by reason of redundancy if it is wholly or mainly attributable to the employer ceasing or intending to cease business, or to the business's requirements for employees to perform work of a particular kind ceasing or diminishing. Tidewell Logistics Ltd informed Mr Achterberg that it had lost its main contract and needed fewer warehouse staff, leading to his dismissal. The dismissal was therefore wholly or mainly attributable to a cessation of business requirements for warehouse operatives.
  > "On 11 September 2026 the company told him it had lost its main contract and needed fewer warehouse staff, and dismissed him for redundancy with immediate effect, without notice."
  authority: uk/ukpga/1996/18/s139

## suitable_alternative: YES [grounded]
Under section 141(2), an employee loses entitlement to a redundancy payment only if he unreasonably refuses an offer of renewal or re-engagement. In this case, the employer made no offer of alternative employment or renewal to Mr Achterberg. Because no offer was made, he did not unreasonably refuse an offer, and he retains his right to a redundancy payment.
  > "He was not offered any other job."
  authority: uk/ukpga/1996/18/s141
