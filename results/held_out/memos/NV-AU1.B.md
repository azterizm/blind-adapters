## dismissed: YES [grounded]
Under section 95(1)(a), the employee is dismissed if the contract is terminated by the employer. The employer dismissed Ms Marchetti with immediate effect, which constitutes termination of the contract by the employer. Section 95(1)(a) applies as the contract was terminated by the employer, and there is no indication that the termination was without notice. The effective date of termination under section 97(1)(a) is the date on which the notice expires, but since the notice was not given, the effective date of termination is the date the termination takes effect, which is the date of dismissal.

## fairness: YES [grounded]
Under section 98(1)(b) of the Employment Rights Act 1996, the employer must demonstrate a substantial reason for dismissal. In this case, the employer dismissed Sofia Marchetti due to her pregnancy, which is a protected characteristic under the Equality Act 2010. The employer's stated reason (maternity leave) does not constitute a fair or substantial reason for dismissal, and the dismissal was not justified under the statutory reasons outlined in section 98(2).
