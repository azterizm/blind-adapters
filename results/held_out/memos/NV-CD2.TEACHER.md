## dismissed: NO [grounded]
Under section 95(1) of the Employment Rights Act 1996, a dismissal occurs if the employer terminates the contract, a limited-term contract expires, or the employee resigns in circumstances justifying constructive dismissal by reason of the employer's conduct. Here, the employer did not terminate the employment contract but exercised a contractual term permitting a shift change on four weeks' written notice. The employee resigned voluntarily because he disliked the new hours without any employer conduct entitling him to terminate without notice, so no dismissal occurred under section 95.

  > "He did not like the new hours and resigned on 14 August 2026, giving one week's notice; his last day was 21 August 2026."
  > "He has no other complaint about the employer."
