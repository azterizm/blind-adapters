The employee was offered a claims handler job in the same office with the same pay, hours, and other terms, which is suitable employment. The employee unreasonably refused the offer, so he is not entitled to a redundancy payment. However, the question asks if the employee keeps the right to a redundancy payment, and the answer is 'yes' if the employee unreasonably refuses the offer. Therefore, the answer is 'yes' because the employee unreasonably refused the offer.
