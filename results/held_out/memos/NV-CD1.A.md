# NV-CD1, arm A: uk_unfair_dismissal
Status: ANSWERED (every step computed or grounded)

## dismissed: YES [grounded]
Under section 95(1)(c), an employee is dismissed if she terminates the contract of employment in circumstances where she is entitled to terminate it without notice by reason of the employer's conduct. Ms Dunmore resigned without notice because the employer demoted her, accused her of dishonesty, and unilaterally changed her contract without her agreement. Under section 97(1)(b), the effective date of termination for a contract terminated without notice is the date on which the termination takes effect.
  > "She resigned without notice on 4 September 2026 because of the demotion and the accusation."
  authority: uk/ukpga/1996/18/s95, uk/ukpga/1996/18/s97

## fairness: YES [grounded]
Under section 98(1), the employer must show a potentially fair reason for dismissal falling within section 98(2) or some other substantial reason. Ashgrove Estate Agents Ltd cannot establish a fair reason for demoting Ms Dunmore, accusing her of dishonesty, and terminating her employment. Furthermore, under section 98(4), the employer acted unreasonably in treating these conduct allegations and demotion as sufficient to dismiss her, rendering the dismissal unfair.
  > "On 1 July 2026 the managing director moved her to a junior negotiator role, took her team away from her and, at a staff meeting, accused her of dishonesty without giving any reason."
  > "Her contract does not allow the employer to change her role and she did not agree to the move."
  authority: uk/ukpga/1996/18/s98
