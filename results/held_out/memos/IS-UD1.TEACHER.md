## fairness: YES [grounded]
The employer can show a potentially fair statutory reason under section 98(2)(b), as alleged misconduct relates to the conduct of the employee. However, under section 98(4), whether the dismissal is fair depends on whether the employer acted reasonably in treating that reason as sufficient to dismiss in accordance with equity and the substantial merits of the case. Because the employer dismissed Ms Nair with no investigation and no disciplinary hearing, it acted unreasonably under section 98(4), rendering the dismissal unfair.
