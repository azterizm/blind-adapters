## dismissed: YES [grounded]
Under section 136(1)(a), an employee is dismissed if the contract under which he is employed is terminated by the employer, whether with or without notice. Northwind Insurance Services Ltd terminated Mr Pryce's employment on 28 August 2026 with written notice. Consequently, he was dismissed within the statutory definition.

## by_reason_of_redundancy: YES [grounded]
Under section 139(1)(b), a dismissal is by reason of redundancy if it is wholly or mainly attributable to the business's requirements for employees to carry out work of a particular kind ceasing or diminishing. Northwind Insurance Services Ltd reduced the Swansea office's claims handler team from ten to six, directly leading to Mr Pryce's dismissal. Therefore, the dismissal was wholly or mainly attributable to a diminution in the business's requirements for employees to perform that work.
  > "On 3 August 2026 the company told him in writing that his team at the Swansea office was being cut from ten claims handlers to six and that his job would end on 28 August 2026."
  authority: uk/ukpga/1996/18/s139

## suitable_alternative: NO [grounded]
Under section 141(1) and (2), an employee is not entitled to a redundancy payment if an offer of suitable alternative employment is made before the end of employment and the employee unreasonably refuses it. The employer offered Mr Pryce a claims handler position in the same office on the same terms starting on 31 August 2026, which was suitable and took effect within four weeks of his employment ending. Mr Pryce refused the offer in writing solely to take a redundancy payment, which constitutes an unreasonable refusal. Consequently, he does not qualify for a redundancy payment under section 141.

  > "He refused the offer in writing on 10 August 2026, saying only that he would rather take a redundancy payment."
