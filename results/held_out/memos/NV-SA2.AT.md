## dismissed: YES [grounded]
Under section 136(1)(a), an employee is dismissed if the contract under which she is employed is terminated by the employer, whether with or without notice. Coastline Travel Ltd gave Ms Hamblin written notice terminating her employment on 30 September 2026. Consequently, she was dismissed by her employer within the statutory definition.
  > "On 1 September 2026 the company gave her written notice that the Plymouth branch would close and that her employment would end on 30 September 2026."
  > "Her employment ended on 30 September 2026."
  authority: uk/ukpga/1996/18/s136

## by_reason_of_redundancy: YES [grounded]
Under section 139(1)(a)(ii), a dismissal is by reason of redundancy if it is wholly or mainly attributable to the employer ceasing or intending to cease carrying on the business in the place where the employee was employed. Coastline Travel Ltd gave Ms Hamblin written notice that the Plymouth branch would close and that her employment would end as a result. Her dismissal was therefore wholly or mainly attributable to the closure of the branch where she worked.
  > "On 1 September 2026 the company gave her written notice that the Plymouth branch would close and that her employment would end on 30 September 2026."
  authority: uk/ukpga/1996/18/s139

## suitable_alternative: YES [grounded]
Under section 141(2), an employee loses entitlement to a redundancy payment only if they unreasonably refuse an offer of suitable employment. The alternative job offered to Ms Hamblin was a junior booking clerk position at a Leeds office, 300 miles away, with a salary a little over half of her current salary. Given the significant difference in role, location, and pay, the offer was not suitable, and her refusal was reasonable.
  > "Before her employment ended it offered her a junior booking clerk job at its Leeds office, about 300 miles away, starting on 5 October 2026, at a salary a little over half of her current salary."
  > "She refused the offer on 8 September 2026."
  authority: uk/ukpga/1996/18/s141
