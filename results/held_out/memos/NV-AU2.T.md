## fairness: UNCLEAR [unclear]
Under section 98(1), the employer must establish the reason for dismissal and demonstrate it is a statutory reason or a substantial reason. The employer dismissed Daniel Okonkwo without providing a reason, which may fail to meet the first stage of the test. However, the employer's failure to provide a reason does not necessarily mean the dismissal is unfair, as the tribunal would need to assess whether the employer could have established a valid reason. The second stage of section 98(4) requires the tribunal to evaluate whether the employer acted reasonably in treating the reason as sufficient for dismissal, which cannot be determined without knowing the reason.
