## dismissed: YES [grounded]
Under section 95(1)(c), the employee terminates the contract due to the employer's conduct (changing shift patterns without notice), which qualifies as a dismissal. Section 97 defines the effective date of termination, which is the date the contract ends, not the date of notice. The employee's resignation on 21 August 2026, after being given notice of shift changes, constitutes a dismissal by the employer under section 95(1)(c).

## automatic_unfair_reason: NO [grounded]
Section 108(3) does not apply because the dismissal was not due to a reason that falls under sections 99, 100, or 103A. The employee resigned due to dissatisfaction with shift changes, which does not meet the criteria for automatically unfair dismissal under those sections. The notice period and resignation date are relevant but do not trigger the automatic unfair dismissal provisions.

## fairness: NO [grounded]
The employer's reason for the change in shift pattern does not fall under the statutory reasons outlined in section 98(2) of the Employment Rights Act 1996. The employer's request to change shift patterns does not relate to the employee's capability, conduct, redundancy, or contravention of an enactment. Therefore, the dismissal does not meet the criteria for a fair dismissal under section 98.
